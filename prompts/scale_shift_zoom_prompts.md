## Produksi Approach A — 5-Stage Match Cut (RECOMMENDED)

**Durasi final: 22 detik** (5 clips × 4s usable + 2s overlap masking)

### Scale Progression

| Stage | Scale | Subject | Clip Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | 10⁻¹⁰ m (atomic) | Atom electron cloud | 5s |
| **2** | 10⁻⁶ m (cellular) | Bacteria swimming | 5s |
| **3** | 10⁰ m (human) | Water droplet on leaf | 5s |
| **4** | 10⁷ m (planetary) | Earth from orbit | 5s |
| **5** | 10²¹ m (galactic) | Milky Way → deep space | 5s |

### Match Cut Rule (KUNCI SEAMLESS LOOK)

Setiap transisi antar clip harus share **1 visual element** yang bridges scale:
- Atom cloud (Stage 1) → bacterium's spherical shape (Stage 2): **lingkaran bulat**
- Bacterium flagella (Stage 2) → water droplet meniscus (Stage 3): **liquid curvature**
- Water droplet reflection (Stage 3) → Earth sphere (Stage 4): **blue sphere**
- Earth curve (Stage 4) → galaxy spiral arm (Stage 5): **cosmic curvature**

Masking: di edit, overlap 0.3-0.5 detik dengan motion blur dip untuk menyamarkan cut.

## 🎞️ Editing Instructions (Do NOT Skip)

### Match Cut Execution

Gunakan **CapCut / DaVinci Resolve / Premiere**. Rule: di titik transisi antara 2 clip:

1. **Last 0.4s of outgoing clip:** tambah motion blur (Directional, 30-50%)
2. **First 0.4s of incoming clip:** tambah motion blur (Directional, 30-50%)
3. **Cross-dissolve 0.3s** antar keduanya — tapi **disguised sebagai motion**, bukan fade
4. Sync audio tail/head dengan rising pitch sweep pada cut point (Freesound: "whoosh rising")

### Color Grading Consistency

Semua 5 stages harus **single LUT** supaya terasa seamless. Rekomendasi:

- **LUT base:** "Teal-Orange Cinematic" standard
- **Lift:** -5 shadows (slight crush)
- **Gamma:** neutral
- **Gain:** +10 highlights (slight bloom)
- **Saturation:** +15 on stages 3-5, natural on 1-2

Export LUT sebagai `.cube` dan apply ke semua 5 clips di Resolve.

### Audio Layering (Wajib untuk "WAH KEREN" effect)

```
Track 1: Generated Veo 3 audio per clip (baseline)
Track 2: Continuous sub-bass drone (20-40Hz sine wave, whole 22s)
Track 3: Orchestral swell (Epidemic Sound: "Cosmic Journey" or similar)
Track 4: Whoosh SFX at each cut point (5 instances)
Track 5: Final chime + silence at end
```

Mix levels: Track 1 at -12dB, Track 2 at -18dB, Track 3 at -8dB peak, Track 4 at -6dB, Track 5 at -3dB.
