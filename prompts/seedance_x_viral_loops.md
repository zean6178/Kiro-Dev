---

## X (Twitter) Analyst Notes

- **Feed is ruthless.** First 0.8s must show motion + face + impact frame. No intro, no logo.
- **Target length: 6–10 seconds.** Seedance 2.0 supports 5s, 10s, 15s — the 10s slot is the sweet spot for X (long enough to breathe, short enough to rewatch).
- **Aspect ratio: 9:16 (vertical) for mobile feed, 1:1 as secondary.** Render wide 16:9 only for cross-post to YouTube Shorts.
- **Loop rule:** Last frame must visually match first frame (same pose, same lighting, same background position). Prompt explicitly says `seamless loop, first and last frame identical`.
- **Hook formula:** impact beat → breath → reset. (e.g. slash → sparks settle → mascot blinks into start pose.)
- **Color rule:** Pick 2 hero colors + 1 accent. Seedance follows palette cues well.
- **Caption strategy on X:** 1 line + 2 hashtags max. Ex: *"He never misses. #AIvideo #anime"*.
- **Posting cadence:** 3×/day, 19:00 / 21:00 / 23:00 WIB. Keep same mascot across the week — audience sticks to characters, not prompts.

# PROMPT-ENGINEERING TIPS SPECIFIC TO SEEDANCE 2.0

1. **Put the action beat in the middle 40% of the prompt.** Seedance weights middle tokens heaviest for motion.
2. **Describe the reset explicitly.** The phrase `returns to the exact original pose` is what creates the loop.
3. **Lock the camera.** Say `static camera` or `slow orbit 360 degrees` — avoid "dynamic handheld" unless you want chaos.
4. **One impact per clip.** More than one hero beat per 10s dilutes the hook.
5. **Palette as two colors + one accent.** `teal-and-peach` > `colorful vibrant rainbow`.
6. **Negative anchors matter.** Always end with `no text, no watermark, no logo, seamless loop`.
7. **Audio is expensive (≈3× credits).** Generate video silent, add SFX later in invideo's free SFX library or CapCut.
