## IMPORTANT REALITY CHECK FIRST

### YouTube Shorts Monetization Requirements (2026)

| Tier | Requirements | What You Unlock |
|---|---|---|
| **Early Access** | 500 subs + 3M Shorts views in 90 days | Super Thanks, Memberships, Shopping |
| **Full Ad Revenue** | 1,000 subs + 10M Shorts views in 12 months | Shorts ad revenue sharing (RPM) |

### Shorts RPM Reality

**Shorts RPM is LOW:** $0.03–$0.10 per 1,000 views across most niches.
**Long-form RPM:** $2–$25 per 1,000 views.

This means: **Shorts alone won't make you rich from ads.** The real money is:
1. Shorts → drive subs → long-form watch hours → high CPM ads
2. Shorts → build audience → affiliate links / sponsors / digital products
3. Shorts → viral reach → brand deals (sponsor CPMs: $15–$150)

**The strategy is: Shorts as a FUNNEL, not a revenue endpoint.**

## IMPORTANT WARNINGS

1. **YouTube banned "low-effort template videos" from monetization in late 2025.** Your clips must have unique style, narrative, or educational value — not just generic AI slop.
2. **Shorts RPM is very low.** Don't expect to live off AdSense at <50K subs. Affiliates and products are your real income.
3. **Consistency beats quality at the start.** Post daily. Improve weekly. Don't wait for "perfect."
4. **Multi-platform from Day 1.** Same clip on YouTube + X + TikTok + IG = 4x reach, same effort.
5. **Long-form is the endgame.** Shorts build audience, long-form (8–12 min) makes money. Plan to add long-form by Month 3.
