# Thumbnail A/B/C Prompts (CTR-Optimized)

> Three variants per film. Each variant targets a different emotional trigger proven to lift CTR on YouTube. Generate each in Midjourney v6 or Flux 1.1 Pro, then A/B test with TubeBuddy or YouTube's built-in test tool.

## Thumbnail Rules (both films)

- **Resolution:** 1280x720, 16:9, under 2MB
- **Face rule:** one face, eyes visible, emotion readable at 120px (mobile size)
- **Color rule:** max 3 dominant colors, complementary contrast (teal/orange or cyan/peach)
- **Text rule:** 3 words max, 120pt+ bold condensed sans-serif, outlined in black, positioned in top-left OR bottom-right (avoid center)
- **Negative space:** 30% of frame reserved for text or YouTube UI overlay
- **Subject placement:** rule of thirds, character face on left third

## CTR Testing Plan

1. Upload all 3 variants as unlisted test thumbnails.
2. Use YouTube's A/B Test tool (available to channels with 1k+ subs) - it auto-rotates for 14 days.
3. Measure **CTR at 24h** and **CTR at 7d**. Pick the higher 7d figure unless 24h is >2x the others (viral signal).
4. If all three are within 0.5% CTR, the **title** is the bottleneck, not the thumbnail. Rewrite titles.
