# Indonesian Nostalgia x Liminal Dreamcore — Short Video Prompt Pack

> **Tujuan:** bikin orang bilang "WAH KEREN" dalam 3 detik pertama, loopable di X,
> re-uploadable di YouTube Shorts. Format: 9:16 vertical, 6–15 detik, audio-native.
>
> **Target:** niche under-served (Indonesian nostalgia + liminal aesthetic) dengan
> kompetisi AI video rendah dan emotional hook tinggi.
>
> **Primary tool:** Google Veo 3.1 (audio native). Fallback: Kling 3.0 + Freesound ambience.

---

## Core Style Bible — Lock Before Generating

Setiap prompt di file ini share 5 ciri konsisten supaya feed kamu punya identitas:

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Aspect ratio** | 9:16 vertical |
| **Duration** | 6–9s (loops) atau 12–15s (mini-narrative) |
| **Film stock look** | Kodak Portra 800 emulation, halation on highlights, 35mm grain medium |
| **Motion** | max slow dolly 5–10mm/s, handheld micro-shake, zero cuts |
| **Lighting** | sickly fluorescent, tungsten warm orange, sodium-vapor yellow, atau moonlit cool blue — **never neutral daylight** |
| **Audio** | ambient first (hum, drone, gamelan kejauhan, adzan), zero music except radio diegetic |
| **Color grade** | slightly faded, lifted blacks, crushed highlights, subtle green-magenta split-tone |
| **Time of day** | dini hari (2–4 AM), senja (5–6 PM), atau malam (10 PM–1 AM) |
| **Typography (text overlay)** | 80s-90s Indonesian TV style, serif tebal atau sans blocky, yellow/white |

**Negative prompt universal:**
```
no modern smartphones, no modern cars post-2005, no digital screens,
no English signage (prefer Bahasa), no daylight blue sky, no people
unless ghostly/silhouette, no fast motion, no music score, no HDR look,
no clean high-contrast render
```

## 1. MALL & KOMERSIAL LIMINAL (8 prompt)

### Prompt 1.1 — "TOKO MAINAN 02:47 AM"
```
9-second cinematic 9:16 vertical video. Empty 1990s Indonesian shopping mall
hallway at 2:47 AM, fluorescent ceiling lights flickering irregularly, polished
terrazzo floor reflecting yellow light, a single metal roll-up shutter
half-closed on an old toy store, faded Bahasa signage "TOKO MAINAN" in chunky

AUDIO: deep electrical hum, distant broken gamelan music on cheap speakers,
one water drip every 3 seconds, low sub-bass drone, echoing footsteps far away
(no visible person).
MOOD: Backrooms aesthetic meets Jakarta 1997. Eerie, melancholic, dreamlike.
NEGATIVE: no people, no modern logos, no daylight, no music score.
Loop seamless.
```

## Production Workflow

### Per-video timeline (target: 15 minutes production)

1. **Pick a prompt** (30s) — rotate through the 30 above
2. **Paste into Veo 3.1 via Google AI Studio** (1 min setup)
3. **Generate 3 variations** (~4 min render)
4. **Pick best take** (1 min)
5. **Minor grade in CapCut** — optional LUT overlay for consistency (3 min)
6. **Export 9:16 MP4 1080p** (2 min)
7. **Dual-upload** — X native video + YouTube Shorts (3 min)

**Total: 15 minutes per post once you have the rhythm.**

### Weekly batching (recommended)

- **Friday night:** generate 14 videos in one session (3 hours)
- **Saturday:** caption all 14 using `x_captions_playbook.md` formula
- **Sunday:** schedule via Hypefury (X) + YouTube Studio (Shorts)
- **Mon–Sun:** sit back, post 2x/day, watch analytics

## Upload Strategy

### X (Twitter)

- **Post times:** 9 AM WIB + 9 PM WIB (catch Asia morning + US evening)
- **Caption formula:** 1 emotional hook Bahasa + 1 English line + 0 hashtags
  - Example: `"jakarta kota, 02:47 pagi. pernah punya kenangan seperti ini?" / "backrooms but make it Indonesian"`
- **Reply boost:** pin a reply with "made with Veo 3, full process in bio" to turn viral into follows

### YouTube Shorts

- **Title:** use Bahasa version of the hook + #shorts
  - Example: `"Mall Matraman jam 2 pagi (AI Generated) #shorts #liminal #indonesia"`
- **Description:** 2 sentences + link to X profile for cross-traffic
- **Thumbnail:** YouTube auto-picks middle frame — our prompts are designed so middle frame = hero shot
