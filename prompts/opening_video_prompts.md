# KARTUN CERDAS — YouTube Opening/Intro Video Prompts

> Production-ready 3D animation prompts for the channel opener, built directly
> from the two reference images: the **"KARTUN CERDAS — BELAJAR JADI SERU"**
> chunky 3D logo and the **Robi** robot mascot.
>
> Target: a **5-second** signature intro bumper that plays before every episode,
> plus a **10-second** extended version for premiere videos.

---

## 1. Design Intent (why this intro works for YouTube)

| Second | What happens | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–0.5 | Robi's face hits frame immediately | Kids recognize mascot → zero drop-off |
| 0.5–1.5 | Robi winks + waves (exact pose from your logo art) | Matches brand art, builds trust |
| 1.5–3.0 | Camera whip-pan, logo crashes in with squash-stretch | Mid-intro energy spike |
| 3.0–4.5 | Logo locks in, "BELAJAR JADI SERU" slides under, yellow star burst | Title card readable for thumbnails |
| 4.5–5.0 | Robi pops up beside logo, thumbs up, sting ends on beep-boop | Memorable sign-off hook |

**Total length:** 5 seconds. **Aspect:** 16:9 (4K master, 9:16 reframe for Shorts).

## 2. MASTER OPENING PROMPT — 5 seconds (universal)

Paste into **Veo 3 / Sora / Kling 2.0 / Runway Gen-3 / Hailuo / Pika**.
Attach both reference images (logo + mascot) when the tool supports image input.

```
A 5-second 3D animated YouTube channel opener for a kids edutainment channel,
Pixar-quality rendering, cinematic, squash-and-stretch animation, 24fps, 16:9.

SETTING: a bright cobalt-blue sky background (#1E5BD8) with soft fluffy white
low-poly clouds drifting slowly, golden sun rays beaming diagonally from the
upper-left, floating yellow sparkle particles and tiny star confetti.

CHARACTER: a chibi 3D cartoon robot named Robi, glossy cloud-white plastic shell
with cobalt-blue accent panels and navy seams, a big black screen-face with two
huge oval cartoon eyes (the left eye winks closed, the right stays wide with twin
highlights), rosy pink cheek blush, red smiling mouth, a yellow spherical antenna
on a short black stem on top of his head, round yellow over-ear headphones with
blue rims, a round yellow play-button icon on his chest inside a blue frame,
short white arms with black articulated elbows, three-fingered white gloves,
stubby white legs with royal-blue soled boots. Adorable, child-safe, highly
expressive.

ACTION TIMELINE:
0.0s – 0.8s : Robi bounces into the center of the frame from below with an
              exaggerated squash-and-stretch jump, landing on an invisible floor
              with a small dust-puff. His right hand raises in a friendly wave,
              his left eye winks, cheek blush pops in with a tiny sparkle burst.
1.0s – 2.2s : Quick zoom-out combined with a horizontal whip-pan motion blur.
              A rainbow streak (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet)
              flies across the screen from left to right leaving a glittery trail.
2.2s – 3.5s : The chunky 3D logo "KARTUN CERDAS" drops in from above with a
              bouncy overshoot, the word "KARTUN" in glossy white with navy
              outline, the word "CERDAS" in bright sun-yellow with navy outline,
              sitting on a cobalt-blue rounded plaque, thick 3D extrusion,
              inner bevel, soft drop shadow. The logo lands with a satisfying
              thud, screen shake micro-bounce.
3.5s – 4.3s : A pill-shaped blue ribbon labeled "BELAJAR JADI SERU" slides in
              from below the logo, white and yellow letters, with two yellow
              sparkle bursts on either side. Yellow stars burst outward around
              the logo like a firework.
4.3s – 5.0s : Robi pops up to the right of the logo, gives an energetic two-
              handed thumbs up, winks again at the camera, final sparkle, freeze
              frame on the locked title card.

COLOR PALETTE: #1E5BD8 cobalt blue, #FFC83D sun yellow, #F7FAFF cloud white,
#FF8FA3 cheek pink, #0E2E6B navy outline.

STYLE: Pixar meets Cocomelon, glossy plastic materials, subsurface scattering,
soft rim light, vibrant saturated colors, joyful kid-friendly lighting, crisp
8K rendering, ultra-smooth motion.

AUDIO CUE (for reference, generate separately): 5-second ukulele + glockenspiel
sting in C major at 95 BPM, with a tiny robot "beep-boop" on the final frame
and a "whoosh + thud" when the logo drops.

NEGATIVE: no scary imagery, no dark tones, no realistic humans, no text
artifacts, no extra limbs, no deformed faces, no uncanny rendering.
```

Use this for the first episode of each season or special uploads.

```
10-second 3D animated YouTube opener for kids edutainment channel "Kartun
Cerdas". Pixar quality, 24fps, 16:9, saturated kid-friendly palette.

          into Robi's yellow play-button chest icon, camera pulls back as Robi

          and detonates into a shower of ROYGBIV bouncy balls that ping-pong
          and dissolve into yellow stars.

4.5–6.5s  Chunky 3D logo "KARTUN CERDAS" crashes down from above with a big
          bouncy overshoot and ground shake — white "KARTUN" + yellow "CERDAS"

6.5–8.0s  The "BELAJAR JADI SERU" pill ribbon slides in from below the logo

          spin, lands with a double thumbs up, winks one eye, cheek blush
```

## 7. SHORTS / VERTICAL 9:16 REFRAME (3 seconds)

For YouTube Shorts and Reels — shorter, taller, more punch.

```
3-second vertical 9:16 opener. Cobalt-blue sky background. Chibi 3D robot
mascot Robi (white-and-blue body, yellow antenna and headphones, yellow play-
button chest, winking left eye, pink cheek blush) jumps into the upper third
of the frame with a huge squash-stretch, waves, winks. The "KARTUN CERDAS"
chunky 3D logo drops into the center third with a bouncy overshoot — white
"KARTUN" + yellow "CERDAS" on a blue plaque. "BELAJAR JADI SERU" pill ribbon
slides into the lower third. Robi pops up beside the logo with a thumbs up,
final sparkle burst. Pixar quality, 24fps, 9:16, squash-and-stretch, saturated
kid-friendly colors #1E5BD8 #FFC83D #F7FAFF. Fast-paced, high energy.

Audio: 3-second zippy ukulele + glockenspiel sting, whoosh + thud + ding.
```

**Shorts safe-zone rule:** keep the logo in the middle 70% of the tall frame — YouTube's UI overlays eat the top 15% and bottom 15%.
