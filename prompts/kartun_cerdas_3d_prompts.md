## 1. YouTube Analyst Brief

### Channel Positioning
| Pillar | Decision |
|---|---|
| Audience | Indonesian children 4–8, co-viewing parents 28–40 |
| Language | Bahasa Indonesia (primary), English sprinkled for bilingual value |
| Niche | STEM + daily life lessons ("kenapa?" / "bagaimana?" questions) |
| Mascot | **ROBI** (short for Robot Cerdas) — the winking blue-white robot |
| Formats | 60–90s Shorts • 5–7 min long-form episodes • 15s intro bumper |
| Upload cadence | 2x Shorts/week + 1x long-form/week (Sunday 7pm WIB) |
| Monetization readiness | YouTube Kids compliant: no scary sfx, no dark themes, faces always visible |

### Retention & Algorithm Rules
- **First 0.5s:** mascot face on-screen, eyes to camera, already moving.
- **First 3s:** a question the kid wants answered (hook).
- **Every 4–6s:** new visual beat (color change, prop swap, camera angle).
- **Every 12–15s:** a "wow" micro-reward (confetti, sparkle, jump cut gag).
- **30s mark:** mini-cliffhanger ("tapi... ternyata...").
- **End:** direct-to-camera call to subscribe + next-video thumbnail hover.
- **Thumbnail:** mascot face 40% of frame, mouth open, one bold Indonesian word (kuning di biru), contrast ratio 7:1.
- **Title formula:** `ROBI Belajar [Topik]! | Kenapa [Pertanyaan]? 🤖✨`
- **Shorts reframe:** 9:16 master cut — keep mascot dead-center, action in upper two-thirds (Shorts UI blocks bottom 15%).

## 5. PILOT EPISODE — "Kenapa Langit Berwarna Biru?" (90s Short)

**Premise:** Robi wakes up, looks up, and asks why the sky is blue. He shrinks
himself, rides a sunbeam, and discovers Rayleigh scattering — explained through
colored bouncy balls.

**Learning objective:** Kids learn that sunlight is made of colors and blue
scatters more in the atmosphere. **Age:** 5–8. **Runtime:** 90s.

### Scene-by-scene video prompts (paste into Sora / Veo 3 / Kling 2.0 / Runway)

#### Scene 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:05)
```
Cinematic 3D cartoon, Pixar quality, extreme close-up of Robi the chibi white-and-
blue robot's face, his big black screen-face fills the frame, left eye winks, right
eye pupil darts up. Dolly back reveals him lying in bright green grass on a sunny
day, blue sky above with fluffy white clouds. He sits up, points at sky, tilts his
head curiously. Colors: sky blue #1E5BD8, grass green, sun yellow. Warm morning
light, shallow depth of field, 24fps, 16:9, child-friendly.
```

#### Scene 2 — QUESTION (0:05–0:12)
```
3D cartoon, medium shot, Robi stands in a cute stylized backyard with cartoon
flowers and a wooden fence. He scratches his round head with his white glove,
a big yellow question mark sparkles above his antenna. He turns to camera and
shrugs with both arms. Bouncy squash-and-stretch animation, 24fps, bright saturated
palette, Pixar-Laika quality, kid-safe.
```

#### Scene 3 — IDEA SPARK (0:12–0:20)
```
3D cartoon, close-up, a glowing yellow lightbulb pops into existence above Robi's
antenna with a "ding" sparkle. His screen-face changes to wide-eyed excitement,
both arms shoot up in the air. Camera does a quick push-in, confetti of tiny yellow
stars. Pixar 3D, squash-stretch overshoot, saturated colors #1E5BD8 blue #FFC83D
yellow #F7FAFF white.
```

#### Scene 4 — SHRINK TRANSFORMATION (0:20–0:30)
```
3D cartoon, Robi presses the yellow play-button on his chest, his body glows,
and with a cute "zwoop" sound effect he shrinks rapidly down to the size of a bee.
Mini-Robi hovers in mid-air, waves his tiny arms, grins. Camera dollies down to
follow him. Radial speed lines, sparkle particle burst, Pixar quality, soft glow.
```

#### Scene 5 — RIDE THE SUNBEAM (0:30–0:45)
```
3D cartoon, wide cinematic shot, mini-Robi surfs on a glowing rainbow-colored
sunbeam streaking down from the sun toward Earth's atmosphere. The sunbeam is
stylized as a ribbon of seven bouncing colored balls: red, orange, yellow, green,
blue, indigo, violet. Robi laughs, arms out. Volumetric god rays, whimsical,
dynamic tracking camera, Pixar 3D, magical, 24fps.
```

#### Scene 6 — THE DEMONSTRATION (0:45–1:05)
```
3D cartoon, Robi lands on a floating stylized platform inside the blue sky. Seven
colored bouncy balls (red to violet) roll toward a transparent wall of tiny white
molecules. The red, orange, yellow balls pass straight through. The blue, indigo,
violet balls bounce wildly in all directions, scattering everywhere. Robi points
excitedly at the blue ball bouncing around. Text caption "BIRU MENYEBAR!" pops up
in chunky yellow 3D letters with navy outline. Pixar quality, clear educational
visual, saturated colors, 24fps.
```

#### Scene 7 — THE AHA MOMENT (1:05–1:18)
```
3D cartoon, Robi zooms back out, returns to full size back in the backyard. He
looks up at the blue sky, now with animated tiny blue sparkles scattering
everywhere across it, visualizing the scattered blue light. He smiles, taps his
chest, winks at the camera. Warm cinematic lighting, Pixar quality, emotionally
satisfying.
```

#### Scene 8 — RECAP + CTA (1:18–1:30)
```
3D cartoon, Robi stands center frame, three floating icons appear around him:
(1) a small sun, (2) rainbow of 7 colored balls, (3) a blue sky with sparkles.
He points to each in order. Text appears in chunky 3D yellow: "CAHAYA = 7 WARNA",
"BIRU MENYEBAR", "LANGIT = BIRU!". Final shot: Robi gives a thumbs up and holds
up a yellow SUBSCRIBE button. Bright Kartun Cerdas brand blue background, confetti
burst, 16:9, Pixar quality, joyful end frame.
```
