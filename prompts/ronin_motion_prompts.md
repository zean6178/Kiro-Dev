## MOTION PROMPT FRAMEWORK

Setiap prompt mengikuti **3-beat structure**:
1. **HOLD** — establish the scene (0–2s)
2. **MOTION** — the cinematic action (2–8s)
3. **SETTLE** — ambient texture / micro-detail (8–10s)

## Prompt Engineering Notes (Dark Photorealistic — different from chibi)

1. **Lead with camera instruction** for live-action style. `Camera begins a slow dolly push-in` tells Seedance exactly what the viewer experiences.
2. **Time-stamp the beats.** Writing `at 8 seconds he is in full close-up` gives the model a timeline.
3. **Name the physics explicitly.** `rain streaks across the frame as particles` / `48fps overcranked slow-motion` → Seedance follows physics cues well.
4. **Restraint in motion = quality.** Static shots with micro-motion (rain, fog, embers) look higher-quality than action because Seedance renders ambient motion more accurately than combat physics.
5. **The crack in the blade (#08) = lore anchor.** Repeat it across future prompts — give the character a story detail and the audience will build narrative around it.
6. **Color grade in the prompt.** `teal shadows + amber highlights` is a Seedance-parseable instruction — it influences the output look significantly.
7. **Negative anchor (always last line):** `no text, no subtitle, no watermark, no logo, no grain filter, no vignette frame` — prevent Seedance from adding default text overlays.
